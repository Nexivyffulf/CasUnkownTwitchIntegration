###### **Installation:**

Add it to "...BepInEx/plugins". Preferably in its own folder for organization.



###### **Usage:**

To enable, find the setting in the settings menu under gameplay. Pressing the button will open a webpage.
There authorize "Grey Planet Live Feed". You will be asked to log into Twitch if you are not already.
If you are a twitch affiliate it should "hopefully" work.



###### **Adding redemptions:**

Look at "Redeems.txt" in there you will see redeems laid out with keywords at the end. You can use them or make your own.
The redeem can be anything you want, it is not case sensitive and can't have ':' in the text parts.
Add it to the list here and on connect a matching one will appear on your channel.
To make it do something, you add the relavent key words to the other side.



give *\<item\>*			--- gives the player the item

give random *\<category\>*		--- gives a random item, optional categories: medical, food, water, tool, drug, container, utility, custom

braindamage			--- causes a random brain damage effect to play

drainbatteries			--- drains your batteries by 33%

teleport			--- teleports you, it is not my fault if this disintegrates you

trip				--- forces you into a ragdoll



###### **Config:**

If you don't trust me with adding and removing these redeems, you can disable it in the config file found in "...BepInEx/configs".
There you will also find a setting to have redeems be disabled instead of being removed.
This is so you can add any pictures or custom colours along with anything else I don't support in Redeems.txt.
The only thing that has to match in Redeems.txt is the title.

There is also 3 other entries that I store stuff in, please don't try to break them.
